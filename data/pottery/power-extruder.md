# Power Clay Extruder: DeWalt Cordless Caulk Gun Conversion

Converting a DeWalt 20V MAX cordless caulk gun into a motorized handheld clay extruder for coils, handles, and other solid forms at Pyns Pottery. The Shimpo handheld extruder does not fit the caulk gun directly, so the conversion uses a 1-1/2" PVC barrel sized like a standard caulk cartridge.

## Compatibility: Shimpo vs. DCE560B

| | Shimpo C-116-91 | DCE560B 10 oz cradle |
|---|---|---|
| Barrel / cartridge diameter | 2-1/4" barrel | Standard 10 oz caulk cartridge (1-3/4" inside diameter) |
| Mounting | Threads into its own ratchet handle | Cradle holds a cartridge; plunger on a toothed rack |
| Direct fit? | No | No |

**Conclusion:** The Shimpo barrel and dies do not drop into the DCE560B. Build a new barrel from 1-1/2" PVC pipe, which matches the size of a standard caulk tube.

## Build Steps

1. **Cut the barrel.** Cut the 1-1/2" PVC to about 8-1/4" long. A standard caulk tube is 8-1/2" long; going slightly shorter lets the plunger enter more easily.
2. **Bevel the loading end.** Lightly bevel the inside of the back (loading) end so clay and the plunger plug slide in cleanly.
3. **Make the die collar.** Attach the coupling/reducer to the non-beveled front end so a die can be trapped between the pipe end and the collar.
4. **Remove and fit the plunger.** Remove the plunger from the gun's toothed rod by loosening the center screw. Grind or sand the plunger edge until it slides freely inside the PVC ID.
5. **Cut the plunger plug.** Use the 1-1/2" hole saw to cut an acrylic disc that sits between the plunger and the clay. This keeps clay off the plunger and rack, the same job the plastic plug in a caulk tube does.
6. **Make dies.** The Shimpo dies are sized for its 2-1/4" barrel and will not seat in the 1-1/2" collar. Either cut new dies from the blank die or acrylic/stainless stock sized to the collar, or turn down existing dies. Put the smooth (chamfered) side of the die facing inward so clay flows through more easily.
7. **Load and run.** Wedge and roll soft clay into a coil slightly smaller than the barrel ID, feed it into the beveled end leaving room for the plug, drop in the plug, set the barrel in the cradle, and extrude.

## Use Notes

- **Clay consistency:** Use soft clay. Clay is far denser than caulk or sealant, and stiff clay can overload or bend the plunger and rack.
- **Run length:** Expect roughly 7 feet of handle-size extrusion per load (reported by another builder with a similar setup).
- **Speed:** Start on the lowest speed dial setting and increase once the extrusion is flowing evenly.
- **Straight extrusions:** Lay the gun on the table and guide the extrusion with the free hand, or hold it vertically and extrude toward the floor.
- **Drip / creep:** DeWalt's cordless caulk guns are known for weaker drip control. Expect a little clay creep after releasing the trigger.
- **Dual use:** The gun still works as a normal caulk gun with the standard 10 oz cradle.

## Cleaning

- Remove the die collar and push remaining clay out with the plunger.
- Let clay dry on dies, then soak so it slakes off.
- Keep the gun body and rack dry; wipe off any slip immediately.
