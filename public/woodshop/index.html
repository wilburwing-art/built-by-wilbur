Compact router shop

Covered: sheet goods, square crosscuts, miters, dados, rabbets, edge profiles, and template work.

Rough lumber: flattening sled for the first face, or buy S4S for face frames and edging. The track saw trues edges with a straight-line rip.

Curves: cut proud with the DCS334B, then flush-trim against an MDF template. A benchtop bandsaw only wins on tight radii and resawing.

Small parts: the weak spot. Plan parallel guides, a coping sled, and push blocks before buying more tools.
