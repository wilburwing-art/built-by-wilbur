Fit, drain and mounting notes

  • Roof: the 130" WB came in low (53") or medium (70") roof per Upfit Supply. The galley's upper tops out near 62", so this van is the medium roof and 70" is the ceiling.
  • Curtain drop: 70" minus about 2" for track, rollers and hooks (est) leaves about 68". Trim the 72" PEVA to about 64" so the hem sits inside the basin.
  • Two curtains: the 45.7" circle is 143.7" around; one 72" curtain covers half. The magnet use that works here is a pair sewn into the overlap where the two meet.
  • Magnets alone do not work: the basin is vinyl, the galley is aluminum, and steel only shows at the transverse roof ribs, so nothing holds a circle.
  • Drain: gravity out the slider door to the ground works. Into the grey tank only if its inlet sits below the van floor. One Amazon reviewer reports the hose connector needs a quick-connect that is not in the box; check on arrival.
  • Track install: KOLAKO reviewers report the coil set is hard to straighten; one mounted theirs to a 1x3 on the ceiling first. A ring of ply or cleats across the ribs does the same job here.
