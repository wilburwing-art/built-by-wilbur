The planned architecture

                                (doubles as a       or Victron Orion-Tr                  │
                                 manual isolator)   Smart DC-DC, 30 A                    ▼
                                                                              6-circuit block (135 A)
starter battery NEG ──4 AWG────────────────────────────────────────────────► house battery NEG
ignition-hot fuse ──12 AWG──► isolator trigger pin

Yeti 12 V out ──► 12-circuit block
  • Breaker before the isolator. The note's own rule: a 100 A breaker between the isolator and the starter battery, so the VSR never has to be trusted as the only disconnect.
  • DC-DC over VSR. The note wants a Victron 30 A charger because the smart alternator's output drops intermittently and a plain VSR would chatter; the Sprinter/Yeti write-up it saved sets the DC-DC's engine-running lockout at 13.2 V / 13.4 V. Whichever is chosen, the trigger comes from an ignition-hot circuit, never from battery voltage alone.
  • Two blocks, two jobs. The house block carries the loads that run with the engine off for hours (Webasto 15 to 20 A, air compressor 18 A, the Yeti charger). The Yeti block carries the cabin loads (fan, 12 V fridge, LED pucks and strips, water pump, exterior lights, a hot-water heater for the shower).

Where the galley circuits land

galley-cabinet.md wires both new circuits straight to the CCP battery-fed studs with inline fuses: the lights branch on 14 AWG through 7.5 A, the pump on 12 AWG through 10 A. That is the right answer today, because it works with nothing else built and the starter battery is the only bank that certainly exists.

Once the Yeti 12-circuit block exists, both move to it: the note already lists "LED strip lights" and "water pump" there, the two inline holders become spares, and the sensors' standby (about 9 Ah a month parked, per the galley doc) stops coming off the starter battery. Two checks before that move:

  • The block cannot exceed the Yeti port it hangs on. Read the Yeti's 12 V output rating first; a pump plus lights plus a fridge on one port is a real sum, and the model is not written down anywhere in the repo.
  • Keep the pump on its own fuse whichever block it lands on; a diaphragm pump's stall current is what the fuse is sized for, not its running draw.
