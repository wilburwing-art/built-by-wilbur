How to read this

  • Fire. FMVSS 302 limits vehicle interior materials to burning no faster than 102 mm per minute. It binds vehicle makers and is the benchmark for a conversion. ASTM E84 "Class A" is a building test, a good sign but a different test.
  • Heat. Cars parked in the sun averaged 116°F inside and 157°F on the dashboard after an hour in an Arizona State study. A material rated to 140°F stays away from glass and the roof skin.
  • Honeycomb needs inserts. A screw pulls out of the thin skins, so every fastener needs a bonded insert and every cut edge a closure.
  • Plastics move. HDPE expands 0.00006 in/in/°F: a 48" panel grows about 1/4" from 70°F to 160°F. Mount it in slotted holes.
  • Blank cells mean the maker's page gives no figure.
