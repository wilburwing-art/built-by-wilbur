Standard cabinets by material: aluminum + Coroplast is about one third the weight of 3/4" ply

One 36" base (34½ H × 24 D, NKBA: 30" box on a 4½" toe kick) and one 36" upper (30 H × 12 D), built four ways. Plywood builds are frameless 3/4" everywhere including the back, with slab doors. The aluminum build is a TNUTZ EX-1010 frame with 4 mm Coroplast skins and doors and a 6 mm Coroplast shelf. Every weight is computed from the parts table below; hinges, pulls, screws, glue, edge banding and finish are left out of every build.

Build notes

  • Coroplast floors and shelves do not carry kitchen loads at a 34" span. A ¼" hardwood veneer-core floor and shelf in the base add about 7.4 lb (0.85 lb/sq ft, Hood table).
  • 4 mm Coroplast doors flex at 18 × 30. 6 mm doors add 1.0 lb a pair; a 1010 frame around each door adds about 8.7 lb a pair of extrusion.
  • Toe kick: the aluminum base has a flush kick panel between the floor and a rail at 4½". A recessed kick needs a stepped front frame and more extrusion.
  • Joints: one CB-010-B inside corner bracket and two COMBO-010-A T-nut screws per rail end. TNUTZ HI-010-A hinges are 0.04 lb each; a Blum-type plywood hinge weight was not found published, so hinges are out of both totals.
  • Tolerances: plywood weight moves with moisture content; Coroplast is ±5% per its spec sheet. The Hood table has no unit label (lb/sq ft fits the magnitudes). The Baltic birch figure is a distributor's listed sheet weight.
