Why DIY builds rarely use aluminum honeycomb

  • Price: Flatiron 4x8 sheets run $679.26 (1/4") to $912.15 (1"), against $86 to $107 for a 5x5 sheet of 18 mm Baltic birch.
  • Fasteners: a screw pulls out of the skin. Every hinge, slide, latch and catch needs a potted or two-piece insert (Flatiron, Chemical Concepts).
  • Cut edges expose open cells and need edge closures; a wrong hole or a dent is permanent.
  • The saving on a van galley is about 16 lb, small next to water and batteries.
  • Best fit: few fasteners, stays put. A countertop or a bed platform; worst fit is a box full of hardware.
