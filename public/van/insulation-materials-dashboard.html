Surface Verdict

Reading the numbers

  • R-values are the maker's or seller's figure at the stated thickness. A van wall never reaches it because the steel ribs conduct around the insulation; filling the cavities to R 5 to 8 behaves more like R 3 to 4 overall. 3M's SM600L data sheet publishes no R-value; the 5.2 is the retailers' figure.
  • Fire: FMVSS 302 is the vehicle test (burn no faster than 102 mm per minute). Thinsulate carries a self-extinguishing rating to it on 3M's sheet. Wool carries a building Class A. Foam plastics are meant to sit behind a barrier, which here is the plywood lining.
  • Moisture decides more than R-value in a steel box. No plastic vapor barrier; pick insulation that sheds water (Thinsulate) or buffers it (wool), and run the vent low at night.
  • Read figures come off the maker's or seller's page. Derived ones are arithmetic from a read figure. Est is a ballpark from a typical density and is colored orange. From memory is an industry figure not re-read today.
