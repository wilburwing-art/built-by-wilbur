Surface Verdict

Reading the numbers

  • Read figures come off the maker's or seller's page. Derived ones are arithmetic from a read figure (a thicker sheet of the same product, or a density). Est is a ballpark and is colored orange.
  • Fire: FMVSS 302 is the vehicle test (burn no faster than 102 mm per minute). ASTM E84 "Class A" is a building test. Untreated plywood carries no rating. Only StarBoard XL claims an FMVSS 302 pass.
  • Kits (Legend, Vans Are Swell) publish no weight. Their $/sq ft is the kit price over the derived area.
