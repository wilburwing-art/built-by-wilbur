What is not counted

  • Not weighed: the owned sink, faucet and tanks, wall and floor lining, the roof fan, wiring, the Victron charger, and anything already in the van that is not listed.
  • Payload default: 3,940 lb is a dealer spec page's figure for a medium roof 130" WB T-250 with the 3.7 L (GVWR 9,000 lb). KBB lists 2016 T-250 payloads from 3,510 to 4,080 lb by trim. NHTSA decodes this van as GVWR class 2G, 8,001 to 9,000 lb.
  • Axle limits: payload is one check. Each axle has its own rating on the same door sticker, and a bed and garage load sit mostly on the rear axle. A truck scale gives both numbers for a few dollars.
  • 150 lb per person is the figure federal tire-load rules use (FMVSS 110: 68 kg per occupant). Enter real weights.
  • Solar panels are not identified; 28 lb for the pair is a from-memory ballpark for two rigid 100 W panels.
