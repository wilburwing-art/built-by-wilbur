8 · Before you buy — checklist

  • SRW confirmed — leaf CK4Z-5560-Q and locker parts below are all single-rear-wheel correct.
  • Snap the door-jamb AXLE sticker to check for a factory LSD ("L" suffix) before buying a Truetrac.
  • Re-verify each price at checkout (dealer vs eBay vs discounter swing $50–100).
  • Front strut: decide bare Motorcraft (Path A, needs spring compressor) vs Bilstein B6 set (Path B).
  • Buy two Bilstein 24-265331 rear shocks — sold individually.
  • Order alignment appointment for after install (control arms + lift both require it).
  • If lifting: TOPO 2.0 includes sway-bar link extensions — confirm they're in the box.
  • Do NOT order Monroe 172393 (Altima) or Bilstein 19-257210 — wrong parts from the old list.

9 · Rear locking axle / traction upgrade

Separate driveline subsystem. This works around traction, not load — it does not replace the SumoSprings/add-a-leaf work above.

Axle confirmed: 2015–2016 Transit-250 RWD uses the Ford 9.75" ring gear, 34-spline rear axle (same family as the F-150 9.75 — aftermarket lists it that way, but ARB & Eaton both explicitly name the 2015–2016 Transit-250). Both carriers below use an all-ratio carrier break, so your gear ratio does not change which part you buy.
Nice to confirm (not a blocker): your door-jamb AXLE code tells you if you already have a factory limited-slip. 31/73/41 = open (3.31/3.73/4.10). 3L/7L/4L = factory LSD — if you see an "L", you may already have traction help and can skip the Truetrac. Not found in your notes; snap the driver door sticker to check.
